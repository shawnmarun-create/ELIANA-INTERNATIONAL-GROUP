Who We Are

An international group serving institutions & communities.

Founded in Canada and operating internationally, Eliana International Group Limited delivers end-to-end procurement, water and logistics services to healthcare providers, governments, mining and industry.

We exist to make institutional supply chains more reliable, more compliant and more humane.

  • Headquartered in Alberta, Canada
  • Three integrated divisions under one group
  • Vetted international supplier network

Founder's Statement

Meet our Founder & CEO.

Founder's
Statement

Fortunate Mugwara believes that true leadership begins with compassion, resilience, and the courage to rise above adversity. Having experienced life's challenges firsthand, she built Eliana International Group Limited with a vision rooted in dignity, excellence, and impact.

Driven by a passion for service and empowerment, Fortunate is committed to creating solutions that improve lives through healthcare support, procurement, logistics, and water supply services. Her mission is not only to build businesses, but to build trust, opportunity, and meaningful change within communities.

Under her leadership, Eliana International Group Limited stands for professionalism, integrity, and purpose-driven growth. Every project, partnership, and service reflects her belief that success should uplift people and create lasting value.

"Success is not only about building wealth — it is about building something that serves people with excellence and purpose."
Fortunate Mugwara
Founder & CEO · Eliana International Group Limited
